Vision

To create a safe and ragging-free environment in the college, where students can pursue their academic goals without fear or intimidation.

Mission

  • To prevent and eliminate ragging in all its forms, and to promote a culture of respect, empathy, and inclusivity among students, faculty, and staff.

Objectives

  1. Prevent ragging: To take proactive measures to prevent ragging incidents and create awareness about its ill effects.
  2. Support victims: To provide support and assistance to students who are victims of ragging.
  3. Promote awareness: To organize awareness programs, workshops, and campaigns to educate students about the consequences of ragging.
  4. Enforce rules: To strictly enforce anti-ragging rules and regulations, and take disciplinary action against those found guilty of ragging.

Outcomes

  1. Ragging-free campus: A safe and ragging-free environment for all students.
  2. Increased awareness: Students, faculty, and staff are aware of the ill effects of ragging and the consequences of indulging in it.
  3. Support system: A support system is in place for students who are victims of ragging.
  4. Disciplinary action: Strict disciplinary action is taken against those found guilty of ragging.
