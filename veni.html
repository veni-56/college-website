About Our College

A.K.D. Dharma Raja Women's College is a renowned Arts & Science institution in Southern India and is affiliated to Madurai Kamaraj University. Established in 1990, to gratify the long-felt educational need of the women of Rajapalayam and neighboring community. The college campus is located amidst the green serenity of the Western Ghats, at Shenbagathoppu Road, Rajapalayam.
