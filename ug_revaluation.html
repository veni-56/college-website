In association with the National Service Scheme (NSS) and Red Ribbon Club (RRC), the Eco Club was established in our college during the academic year 2014–2015. The club operates with the inspiring motto: “What you do will come back to you tomorrow.” Nearly 100 students have actively joined as members of the Eco Club. The primary aim of the club is to raise awareness about environmental protection, promote clean and green surroundings, and encourage tree plantation to save our earth. Students gather once a week to discuss and implement environmental action plans and awareness campaigns.

Vision

To build a generation of eco-conscious individuals who understand the value of a clean, green, and sustainable environment.

Mission

  • To promote environmental awareness among students and the community.
  • To encourage tree plantation, waste reduction, and eco-friendly practices.
  • To cultivate habits of cleanliness, conservation, and sustainability.
  • To engage students in hands-on environmental activities and service.
